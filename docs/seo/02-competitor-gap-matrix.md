## Expected gap themes (to confirm)
1. **Credit-check clarity**: soft vs hard search, CCJs, defaults, rejection. Existing pages cover part; no dedicated "no credit check" explainer.
2. **Calculators with depth**: 11 calculators exist; check whether competitors show APR/term comparison tables and shareable results (this site does).
3. **Treatment coverage**: no Invisalign, dentures, bridges or emergency-treatment finance pages.
4. **Trust signals**: operator identity, FCA status and commission disclosure are missing here (see 08). Competitors that are authorised lenders show these prominently.
5. **Freshness**: 109 of 121 pages are dated May 2026 or earlier (`01-url-audit.md`).
