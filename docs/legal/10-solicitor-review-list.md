1. **Perimeter:** confirm that the current site (education, generic calculators, provider profiles, general enquiries) involves no regulated activity and no unapproved financial promotion. Advise on any wording or layout changes.
2. **Provider profiles and comparisons:** do they "invite or induce" under s21/FPO? Are the disclosures adequate? Is the CAP comparison treatment adequate?

7. **Terms:** governing law and jurisdiction (deliberately omitted), liability wording, consumer-rights statements.
8. **Corporate details:** registry confirmation of name, MERSİS, address, VKN and tax office before VKN or tax office are published.
