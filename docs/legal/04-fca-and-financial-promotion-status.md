# 04. FCA authorisation and financial promotion status

| Question | Answer (current) |
|---|---|
| Is Multiligo FCA-authorised? | **No** (owner-confirmed). Not checked on the FCA Register from this environment. |
| Appointed representative of an authorised firm? | No arrangement known. |
| Any s21-approved financial promotions? | **None.** No approver relationship exists. |
| Regulated credit activity carried out? | Intended: **none.** No introductions, applications, eligibility tools or finance affiliate links. |
| What the site may do on this basis (subject to solicitor confirmation) | Publish neutral education, generic illustrative calculators and descriptive provider information with disclosures; take general enquiries without passing them to lenders. |
| What it must not do | Introduce users to lenders or brokers, promote specific credit products as an invitation or inducement, collect finance applications, simulate approvals or run finance affiliate links, unless authorised or an AR or approval route is in place. |
| Site wording | Pages state "not authorised by the FCA"; no "FCA approved" or "regulated" claims (tested). |

The website must not be described as exempt. If commercial finance features are wanted later, the routes to evaluate with a solicitor are: becoming an AR of an authorised principal, direct authorisation, or having promotions approved by a permitted s21 approver. Each brings conduct obligations, including CONC and the Consumer Duty for the authorised party.
