# 01. UK consumer credit regulatory perimeter assessment

**Draft for solicitor review. Not verified against current legislative text (sources were unreachable from this environment).**

## Facts relied on (owner-confirmed, 2026-10-16)
- Operator: Multiligo Reklam Bilişim Pazarlama ve Danışmanlık Ltd. Şti., Türkiye. Not FCA-authorised. No appointed-representative arrangement known.
- The site publishes educational guides, illustrative calculators and provider profiles. There are no affiliate links and no lender links.
- WhatsApp (+90 535 399 8999) and the contact form are answered by the Multiligo team. Enquiries are **not passed to lenders or brokers**.
- Multiligo receives **paid marketing service fees from some dental clinics**, including clinics in Türkiye. No payments come from lenders, brokers or finance providers.
- The site is aimed at UK readers.

## Framework to confirm (verify current text)
- **FSMA 2000 s19** (general prohibition) and **s21** (financial promotion restriction).
- **RAO 2001 art 36A** credit broking. This covers effecting introductions of individuals to persons who enter into, or broker, regulated credit agreements, and certain acts preparatory to them. It also covers related exclusions and the "by way of business" test. Check PERG 2 and PERG 2.7.7B onwards for the FCA's view on introductions, online lead generation and websites.
- **FPO 2005**: entering into regulated credit agreements is a controlled activity, so communications that invite or induce someone to take credit can be financial promotions.
  - **Art 12 (overseas communicators)** is not available for communications directed at UK persons.
  - **Art 20 (journalists)** is limited and conditional.
- **s21 approver gateway** (in force since February 2024): approval of an unauthorised person's promotion requires an approver with the relevant FCA permission.
- **CONC 3** (financial promotions) applies to authorised firms. It also matters because an approved promotion must comply with it.
- **Consumer Duty** (PRIN 2A) applies to authorised firms only, so not to the current model.
- **UK GDPR art 3(2)** applies to a non-UK controller that offers services to, or monitors, people in the UK. **Art 27** requires a UK representative unless an exception applies. Also relevant: **DPA 2018**, **PECR reg 6** (cookies) and the data protection fee regime.
- **CAP Code**: rules 2.1 to 2.4 (recognisability, affiliate marketing), section 3 (misleading advertising and comparisons), section 14 (financial products) where relevant.
- **DMCC Act 2024 Part 4 Chapter 1** (unfair commercial practices, from April 2025), including the banned practices in Schedule 20 such as fake or misleading reviews.
- Insurance distribution (RAO art 21, 25 and 39A to 39L) is not engaged: there are no insurance products.

## Activity-by-activity view
See `02-risk-matrix.md` for the classification of all 18 activities. Summary:
- **Likely outside the perimeter (subject to confirmation):** neutral educational articles, terminology, and generic illustrative calculators with no product named.
- **Possible financial promotion (needs review):** provider profiles and comparisons that name lenders, and "0%" or rate discussions tied to named providers. Content is designed to describe, not invite or induce. Whether a page "invites or induces" is fact-specific, and a disclaimer does not change it.
- **Possible credit broking:** none in the current build. Any future step that introduces users to lenders or brokers would need authorisation or an AR arrangement before launch: passing details, lead forms aimed at finance, finance affiliate links (even with no fee, since "by way of business" and remuneration both matter).
- **Clinic referral for a fee** is not a credit activity in itself. It is a commercial relationship that must be disclosed (CAP, DMCC), and the data flows must be covered by privacy notices.
- **Treatment abroad:** no UK credit regulation is engaged by information alone. It must not imply that UK finance products can be used overseas.

## Conclusion (provisional)
The current build is intended to sit outside regulated credit activity:
- No introductions to lenders.
- Finance data removed from WhatsApp messages.
- Provider pages limited to description with a comparison disclosure.

The following remain open for the solicitor:
- Confirm that the provider profiles and comparison pages are not financial promotions, or what changes would make them so.
- Confirm the art 27 position and the data protection fee position for a Türkiye-based controller.
- Confirm that marketing-fee arrangements with clinics, combined with clinic-matching enquiries, create no other regulated or consumer-law obligations (for example healthcare advertising rules).
