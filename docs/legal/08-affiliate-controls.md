# 08. Affiliate and monetisation controls

Statuses: RESEARCH, REGULATORY REVIEW, COMMERCIAL REVIEW, APPROVED, ACTIVE, BLOCKED. **No partner is ACTIVE.**

| Partner / type | Category | Status | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Any lender or credit broker affiliate (e.g. those profiled on provider pages) | Credit | **BLOCKED** | Possible credit broking and financial promotion; operator not authorised |
| Any dental insurance or plan affiliate | Insurance or plan | **BLOCKED** | Insurance distribution; financial promotion |
| Dental clinics paying Multiligo for marketing | Clinic marketing | Existing relationship, **disclosed** | Not a finance arrangement; solicitor to review clinic-referral model and healthcare advertising rules |

Activation rules:
1. No partner moves past REGULATORY REVIEW without written advice covering credit broking, s21, insurance distribution, data sharing and CAP disclosure.
2. An authorised partner does not authorise Multiligo's own activity.
3. Disclosures must appear at the link, not only in the footer.
4. Partner links must not carry personal data in URLs.
